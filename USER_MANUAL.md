# User Manual

Subscription Tracker helps users manage recurring subscriptions, bills, payments, reminders, reports, and account settings in one dashboard.

## Getting Started

1. Open the application in your browser.
2. Sign in with your account credentials.
3. After signing in, use the left sidebar to navigate through the available sections.

## Dashboard

The Dashboard is the main landing area after sign-in. It is intended to summarize current bills, active subscriptions, upcoming reminders, recent payments, and overall spending activity.

## Bills

Use Bills to review and manage recurring or one-time bills such as utilities, rent, insurance, loan payments, or service invoices.

Recommended bill details include:

- Bill name
- Amount
- Due date
- Billing frequency
- Payment status
- Notes or provider information

## Subscriptions

Use Subscriptions to track recurring services such as streaming platforms, software tools, cloud services, memberships, and app plans.

Recommended subscription details include:

- Service name
- Monthly or annual cost
- Renewal date
- Billing cycle
- Category
- Cancellation or renewal notes

## Payments

Use Payments to review completed or scheduled payments. This area helps verify whether a bill or subscription has already been paid and supports future reporting.

## Analytics

Analytics is intended to show spending trends, recurring cost totals, category breakdowns, and changes over time.

## Reminders

Use Reminders to track upcoming due dates and renewal dates. Reminders should help prevent missed payments and surprise renewals.

## Search Records

Use Search Records to find bills, subscriptions, payments, and reports quickly by keyword, provider, category, date, or status once those filters are implemented.

## Reports

Reports is intended for exporting or reviewing financial summaries, recurring cost reports, payment histories, and account-level activity.

## Settings

Use Settings to manage account preferences, notification preferences, display options, and application configuration when those features are implemented.

## Signing Out

Select Logout from the lower section of the sidebar. The application clears the current session and returns to the login screen.

## Data Safety Tips

- Do not store banking passwords in notes fields.
- Review renewal dates regularly.
- Keep account access limited to trusted users.
- Report suspicious behavior to the project maintainers using the process in `SECURITY.md`.
