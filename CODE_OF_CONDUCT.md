# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, and productive project environment for contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and users.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate with respect and patience.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Welcome different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Ask clarifying questions before assuming intent.
- Respect project scope, review decisions, and maintainer guidance.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, or personal attacks.
- Public or private intimidation.
- Sharing another person’s private information without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that prevents productive collaboration.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer instructions or repository rules.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject merge requests, restrict access, or take other appropriate action when behavior violates this code of conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitLab project channels. Include relevant context, links, screenshots, and the names of involved parties when appropriate.
